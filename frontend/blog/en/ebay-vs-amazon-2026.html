eBay vs Amazon:
Which Platform in 2026?

The complete comparison to pick the marketplace that maximizes your margins — and avoid the traps.

eBay or Amazon? It's the question thousands of sellers ask before starting. Both giants have strengths and weaknesses, and the right choice depends on your product, your budget, and your strategy.

Fees: a tight match

On paper, both platforms charge similar final value fees: 8–15% depending on the category. But the real difference lies elsewhere:

  • eBay: fee + payment processing (~2%). Typical total cost: 10–17%.
  • Amazon: fee + processing. If you use FBA (fulfillment by Amazon), add storage and shipping fees that can push the total cost well beyond 20%.

Selling yourself (FBM) on Amazon keeps you comparable to eBay. With FBA, you pay more but outsource logistics.

Audience: Amazon wins on volume

Amazon attracts considerably more traffic, especially for new, mainstream products. eBay keeps a loyal base of bargain hunters, used-goods buyers, collectors, and spare-parts shoppers. If your product is new and standardized, Amazon offers more potential volume. If it's niche, used, or unusual, eBay is often more relevant.

Competition: Amazon is saturated

Amazon has become extremely competitive: thousands of sellers fight over the same keywords, often with fierce price wars. eBay, while competitive, leaves more room for specialized sellers and differentiated products.

Seller experience

  • eBay: simpler to start, fewer constraints, ideal for launching or testing a product.
  • Amazon: more rules, a more demanding seller account, but powerful tools (FBA, Brand Registry) if you're aiming for volume.

So, eBay or Amazon?

In short: Amazon for volume on new, standardized products; eBay to start fast, sell used or niche products, with a gentler learning curve. Many smart sellers use both in parallel to diversify their income.

⚖️ Our verdict

There's no absolute winner: Amazon shines on volume, eBay on accessibility and niches. Whatever you choose — or if you choose both — one thing is certain: you must know your exact fees to know whether you're actually making money.

❓ Frequently asked questions

Is it cheaper to sell on eBay or Amazon?

Fees are similar (8–15%). Amazon costs more if you use FBA (storage + shipping). With direct shipping (FBM), both stay comparable.

Can I sell on eBay and Amazon at the same time?

Yes, it's a common strategy to diversify income and audiences. You just need to manage inventory and margins carefully.

Which platform is best to start with?

eBay is often more accessible to start: fewer rules, fast onboarding, and a good place to test a product before scaling.
