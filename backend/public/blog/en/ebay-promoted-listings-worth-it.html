eBay Promoted Listings:
Are They Actually Worth It?

Understand the real cost of eBay advertising before you tick the "Promoted" box.

You've probably seen the "Promoted Listings" checkbox when creating a listing. eBay promises more visibility, and therefore more sales. But behind that promise is a cost many sellers underestimate.

So, is eBay advertising an investment or a margin leak? A numbers-based answer.

How do Promoted Listings work?

Promoted listings appear in better positions in search results. Unlike classic advertising, you only pay if the buyer clicks and then buys: eBay takes a percentage of the sale price (the "ad rate") on top of the normal final value fee.

How much does it actually cost?

The ad rate is dynamic and depends on competition, but it usually sits between 2% and 10% of the sale price. It stacks on top of the final value fee (8–15%) and payment fees (~2%). On a €50 product, a 5% Promoted Listing costs you €2.50 more — on top of the €5–8 eBay already takes.

In total, a promoted sale can cost you 18–25% of the price. That's a lot.

The profitability calculation you should run

Advertising is only profitable if the sale wouldn't have happened without it. Ask yourself three questions:

  • What is my net margin on this product (after eBay fees)?
  • Does the ad rate still leave me an acceptable margin?
  • Would this product sell just as well without promotion?

A simple rule: if your net margin is under 15–20%, avoid promotion. If it's above 30% and the product is buried in a competitive category, advertising can be a powerful lever.

When it's worth it

  • High-margin products (over 30%)
  • Highly competitive categories where you need to stand out
  • Launching a new product with no sales history

When it's not worth it

  • Low-margin products or ones already ranking well organically
  • Items with a high return rate
  • When you don't actually measure your real fees

⚖️ Our verdict

Promoted Listings are a tool, not a requirement. They only become profitable when your margin allows it and the extra visibility offsets the cost. Before you promote, know your exact fees: that's the only way to decide with confidence.

❓ Frequently asked questions

How much does an eBay Promoted Listing cost?

The ad rate is dynamic and usually ranges from 2% to 10% of the sale price, charged only when a click leads to a sale.

Can you lose money with Promoted Listings?

Yes, if the extra ad cost exceeds your net margin. On low-margin products, promotion can turn a sale into a loss.
