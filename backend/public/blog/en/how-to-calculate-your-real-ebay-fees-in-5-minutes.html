How to Calculate Your Real eBay Fees
in 5 Minutes (Even with Promotions)

9 out of 10 sellers underestimate their real fees. Discover the 3‑step method to know your exact costs and never sell at a loss again.

9 out of 10 eBay sellers underestimate their real fees. Between variable commissions, storage fees, listing upgrades, and VAT, the amount eBay deducts is often much higher than you think. The result? You might be selling at a loss without realizing it.

Here is a 3‑step method to find out your true costs in under 5 minutes, using a concrete scan of your store powered by BeassistScan.

📌 Step 1: Identify the 4 fee categories that add up

Before calculating, you need to know what to look for. eBay charges much more than just the final value fee:

  • Insertion fees: the cost to list your item (free up to a certain number, then paid).
  • Final value commission: variable percentage (from 6% to 15% depending on the category).
  • Storage & logistics fees (eBay Fulfillment) if you use their warehouses.
  • Paid options: promoted listings, subtitles, premium gallery, etc.

Note: one‑off promotions and subscription discounts make these amounts highly volatile. Reading the official price tables is not enough.

⏳ Step 2: The manual method (the one wasting your time)

To calculate manually, you would need to:

  1. Download your eBay sales report (CSV format).
  2. Extract every single commission and optional fee line by line.
  3. Apply local VAT rates (20% in the UK, 19% in Germany, etc.).
  4. Subtract any discounts or rebates provided by eBay.

⏱ Estimated time: between 45 minutes and 1 hour for 100 sales, with a 30% risk of miscalculation.
