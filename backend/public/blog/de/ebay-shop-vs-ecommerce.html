eBay Store vs Independent E-commerce
The Complete 2026 Comparison

Commissions, visibility, independence, customer loyalty, hidden costs… Everything you need to decide which platform will make you more money.

You've built a successful eBay store. The traffic is there, the sales are coming in — but the fees keep piling up. Is it time to launch your own e-commerce site?

This is one of the most common questions among growing eBay sellers. The answer isn't black and white — it depends on your catalog size, target audience, and long-term goals.

In this article, we compare every key dimension so you can make an informed decision.

💰 The Fee Math: Where Does Your Money Go?

Let's take a seller with $50,000 annual revenue:

  • eBay: $6,000–$7,500 in commissions + $336 subscription + Promoted Listings = up to $10,000+
  • Own store (Shopify/WooCommerce): $600 hosting + $750 payment fees (1.5%) = ~$1,350

The difference? Up to $8,650 per year — money that stays in your pocket with an independent store.

📈 Traffic: eBay's Biggest Advantage

eBay brings you immediate, free traffic from 135 million active buyers. This is its strongest card — and why sellers stay despite the high fees.

An independent store starts at zero traffic. You need SEO, social media, paid ads, or email marketing to drive visitors. This takes time and expertise — but once built, it costs far less per visitor than eBay's implied traffic cost.

💡 The smart strategy: Don't choose one or the other. Start on eBay to build cash flow, then use those profits to invest in your own store. Run both in parallel, then gradually shift your focus as your independent site grows.

🎯 Customer Loyalty & Data: A Game-Changer

On eBay, buyers belong to eBay — not to you. You can't email them, retarget them, or build a relationship beyond the transaction.

With your own store, every buyer is your customer. You can build an email list, create loyalty programs, send personalized offers, and achieve repeat purchase rates of 30–40% (vs. 5–10% on eBay).

⚠️ Platform Risk: The eBay Dependency Problem

In 2026, many eBay sellers have seen their accounts restricted or suspended with little warning. If eBay is your only sales channel, this represents an existential risk to your business.

An independent store gives you full control — no algorithm changes, no arbitrary fee increases, no suspension risk.

✅ When to Stay on eBay

  • You're just starting out and need immediate sales
  • You sell in niche categories with strong eBay demand
  • You lack the technical resources to build and market a store
  • Your catalog is very large and eBay search handles discovery well

✅ When to Launch Your Own Store

  • You're paying over $3,000/year in eBay fees
  • You have a recognizable brand or niche
  • You want to build customer relationships and loyalty
  • You want to reduce platform dependency risk
  • Your margins are being crushed by Promoted Listings

❓ Frequently Asked Questions

Can I sell on eBay and have my own store at the same time?

Absolutely — and this is the recommended approach. Many successful sellers use eBay for discovery and their own store for repeat customers and higher margins.

How long does it take to build an independent e-commerce store?

With platforms like Shopify or WooCommerce, a basic store can be live in 1–2 weeks. A fully optimized, well-marketed store typically takes 3–6 months to gain real traction.

What percentage of my eBay catalog should I migrate?

Start with your top 20% of products (by revenue) — they likely drive 80% of your income. Migrate these first and focus your marketing there before expanding.
